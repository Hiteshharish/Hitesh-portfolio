Bewell

Personal — Concept Application

An AI-powered wellness hub that unifies fragmented health data — wearables, lab results, medications, and mental health check-ins — into one clear, actionable dashboard. Designed for progressive disclosure, privacy, and daily habit formation.

Overview

Most people who track their health end up with data scattered across a dozen apps — a step counter here, lab results in a portal there, a meditation streak somewhere else. Bewell is a concept exploring what it would look like to bring all of that into one thoughtful, calm, and genuinely useful experience.

This is a personal concept project — not a client engagement. It's a space to explore ideas around health UX, AI-generated plain-language summaries, and progressive disclosure in high-stakes information environments.

The Problem Space

Health data is increasingly available but rarely actionable. Users are overwhelmed by raw numbers without context, worried about privacy, and unable to see the connections between different aspects of their health. The result: most health apps are opened once and abandoned.

"What if your health data gave you one clear thing to focus on today — instead of twenty metrics to worry about?"

Design Highlights

  • Personalised health score

    A single, contextualised score derived from multiple data sources — with plain-language explanation of what's driving it.

  • AI insight summaries

    AI-generated summaries that translate clinical data into everyday language — without oversimplifying or causing alarm.

  • Contextual habit tracking

    Reminders based on your patterns, not a generic schedule. If you sleep late, your morning nudge adjusts accordingly.

  • Progressive disclosure

    Complexity is always available — but never forced. Depth is one tap away, not the default view.

  • Privacy-first data model

    Explicit data consent at every step. What's shared, with whom, and for what — always visible and always reversible.

Key Design Decisions

Gentle nudges over overwhelming metrics

The dashboard leads with one recommended action, not a wall of numbers. Everything else is available on request.

Accessible colour for health status

Status indicators never rely on colour alone — always paired with shape, pattern, or label.

One daily summary

A single notification per day — not a stream. Reduces anxiety, increases signal-to-noise ratio.

Full WCAG AA compliance

Health tools serve people who may be stressed, distracted, or living with disabilities. Accessibility isn't optional here.
