Bewell

Personal — Concept Application

An AI-powered wellness hub that unifies fragmented health data — wearables, lab results, medications, and mental health check-ins — into one clear, actionable dashboard. Designed for progressive disclosure, privacy, and daily habit formation.

Overview

Most people who track their health end up with data scattered across a dozen apps — a step counter here, lab results in a portal there, a meditation streak somewhere else. Bewell is a concept exploring what it would look like to bring all of that into one thoughtful, calm, and genuinely useful experience.

This is a personal concept project — a space to explore ideas around health UX, AI-generated plain-language summaries, and progressive disclosure in high-stakes information environments.

"What if your health data gave you one clear thing to focus on today — instead of twenty metrics to worry about?"

Design Highlights

→Personalised health score — a single contextualised score with plain-language explanation of what's driving it
→AI insight summaries — clinical data translated into everyday language without oversimplifying or causing alarm
→Contextual habit tracking — reminders based on your patterns, not a generic schedule
→Progressive disclosure — complexity always available, never forced; depth is one tap away
→Privacy-first data model — explicit consent at every step, always visible and reversible
→WCAG AA compliance throughout — including accessible colour for health status indicators
