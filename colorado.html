Colorado Peak

Deloitte Consulting — Internal Pitch

Reimagining how citizens of Colorado access critical state services — benefits, healthcare, employment — through a more human-centred, connected system built on accessibility, plain language, and trust.

Overview

Colorado's Peak platform served millions of citizens navigating complex benefit systems — from healthcare enrollment to unemployment insurance. Despite the critical importance of these services, the legacy interface created unnecessary friction, confusion, and inequitable access for the people who needed help most.

This was a Deloitte internal pitch: a team of designers proposing a vision for how a modern civic service platform could work — grounded in real research and built around human needs, not bureaucratic process.

The Challenge

"Case workers told us they spent up to 40% of their time helping users find the right form — not completing the form."

The legacy interface created unnecessary friction and confusion. Case workers spent significant time helping users with basic navigation rather than providing substantive support.

Our Approach

We conducted extensive field research with case workers and citizens — identifying real pain points, language barriers, and accessibility gaps in the existing system.

From this foundation, we proposed a unified service portal that reduced cognitive load, used plain language throughout, and met WCAG AA standards. The design centred equity: ensuring that citizens most in need could access services without friction or fear.

Methods

→Field interviews with citizens & case workers to map the real experience
→Affinity mapping — synthesised 200+ observations into design-driving themes
→Service blueprinting to expose systemic gaps front-stage and back-stage
→Prototype testing with representative users across age groups and literacy levels
→Accessibility audit — WCAG AA compliance throughout
